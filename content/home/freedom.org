RNT and Sakura are free software under the GNU Affero General Public
License, version 3, and Sol under the GNU GPL, version 3. Run them for any
purpose, study how they work, change them, and share them, with your changes
or without.

The Affero clause is the part we care most about for a database kernel: run
a modified RNT as a network service, and everyone who uses it is entitled to
its source too. Relations should not be locked away, and neither should the
software that keeps them.
