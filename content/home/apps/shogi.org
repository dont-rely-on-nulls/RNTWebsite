A capture moves a tuple from =PIECE= into the captor’s =HAND=, and a drop
moves it back onto the board, on the other side. The rules that make shogi
shogi are requirements the kernel checks on every move, and tsume problems,
mate in /n/ from a given position, are a search Tangram runs into one
branch per solution.

#+begin_src aleph
-- nifu: never two unpromoted pawns of one side on a file
require nifu:
  always not (PIECE(at: a, kind: 'pawn', side: s, file: f)
          and PIECE(at: b, kind: 'pawn', side: s, file: f) and a != b)
#+end_src
