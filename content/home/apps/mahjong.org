Four players are four sessions, and each sees its own hand and nobody
else’s: hidden information is just a question of who may read which
tuples. Karuta decides whether a hand is complete, four sets and a pair,
and Omikuji says how likely it is that the tile you are waiting for is
still in the wall.

#+begin_src omikuji
-- an unseen tile is equally likely to be anywhere unseen
weigh WALL(tile: t) by UNSEEN(tile: t, copies: n, hidden: h) as n / h.

-- is my wait still live?
ask chance WALL(tile: '5-pin').
#+end_src
