Every point is a tuple of =POINT (AT, COLOUR)=, and the grid is a relation
of neighbours. A group is a Dandelion closure over neighbours of the same
colour, its liberties are the empty points it touches, and a group left
without any is taken off in the same commit. Ko is a hash the game has seen
before, and Soroban sums influence across the board for the score.

#+begin_src dandelion
ally:
  ?A  NEXT    ?B.
  ?A  COLOUR  _c.
  ?B  COLOUR  _c.

? liberties of the group at D4:
  'D4'    ally*   _s.
  _s      NEXT    ?POINT.
  ?POINT  COLOUR  'empty'.
#+end_src
